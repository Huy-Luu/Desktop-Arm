# TICKET-10: FreeCAD Rack-and-Pinion Gripper & Wrist Linkage

- **Status**: Ready
- **Blocked By**: None
- **Delivers**: Symmetrical dual-rack and pinion parallel gripper assembly driven by an MG90S micro-servo with silicone finger pads and wrist pitch/roll mounting bracket.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Module 0.8 spur pinion gear mounted to MG90S servo horn.
2. Opposed linear gear racks sliding smoothly along integrated dovetail or cylindrical guide rails.
3. Maximum grip aperture $\ge 45\text{mm}$, closing down to $0\text{mm}$ with rubber/silicone pad recesses.
4. Total end-effector assembly weight $<70\text{g}$ to minimize shoulder cantilever load.
